Send the request from the email address associated with your Binder account when possible. Do not send identity documents, passwords or payment information.

Request account deletionRead retention details

What happens

  1. Your request is matched to the Binder account.
  2. The product account is deactivated so it stops appearing in discovery and active conversations.
  3. Account-associated product data and authentication identity are deleted through the deletion workflow.
  4. If a narrowly defined safety, fraud-prevention or legal record must be retained, it is minimized and kept outside normal product use as described in the privacy policy.
Security note. Binder will never ask for your password in a deletion request. If account ownership cannot be verified safely, we may ask for a confirmation from the account email address.

In-app deletion

Signed-in users can also start deletion from Binder’s Profile → Account controls. The public resource remains available for people who have uninstalled or lost access to the app.

Response times can depend on account verification and legitimate retention requirements. This page is the designated public deletion resource for Binder.
