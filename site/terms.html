Terms & community rules

Mutual interest does not remove boundaries.

These rules apply before you create a Binder profile, upload a photo or send a message.

Version: 2026-08-15 · Effective: 15 August 2026

Binder is 18+ only. Do not use Binder if you are under 18, and do not misrepresent your age or identity.

1. Respect and consent

You may not use Binder to threaten, harass, stalk, shame, intimidate or pressure another person. A match is permission to start a conversation, not permission for sexual conduct, persistent contact or offline access.

2. Prohibited content and behavior

  • sexual exploitation or abuse of children, grooming, or any sexualized content involving minors;
  • sexually explicit or pornographic profile content;
  • credible threats, incitement to violence, hate or targeted harassment;
  • non-consensual intimate imagery or sexual coercion;
  • impersonation, deceptive identity claims, scams, fraud or coordinated spam;
  • sale, facilitation or promotion of illegal goods or activity;
  • publishing another person’s private information without permission;
  • attempts to bypass blocks, moderation, rate limits or account restrictions.

3. Profile and message responsibility

You are responsible for content you upload or send. Use images you have the right to use. Do not include misleading claims that materially misrepresent who you are.

4. Safety tools

Binder may provide report, block, unmatch, rate-limit and moderation controls. We may preserve relevant evidence snapshots when a safety report is submitted and may restrict or remove content or accounts to protect users and the service.

5. Moderation

Content may be queued, limited, rejected or removed. Accounts may be suspended for credible safety concerns, repeated violations or attempts to evade enforcement. Serious illegal or child-safety matters may require escalation consistent with applicable law.

6. Account deletion

You can request deletion from the app or from Binder’s public deletion resource. Data that must legitimately be retained for safety, fraud prevention or legal compliance will be minimized and handled separately from ordinary product use.

7. Beta service

Binder is under active development. Features can change, and availability is not guaranteed. The initial core is presented as free; future monetization should not retroactively misrepresent the current product.

8. Contact

Questions or safety concerns: nullmesh@protonmail.com.
