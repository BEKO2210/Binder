Product discipline

The useful product stays free.

Binder is intentionally focused: identity, discovery, mutual matching, conversation, safety, settings and profile control. There is no Pro plan, paid boost or artificial feature lock in the product roadmap.

01 · DISCOVER

Fit both sides.

Age, preferences and distance are evaluated server-side for both people — not just yours. Exact coordinates never enter another client’s payload.

02 · BIND

One durable decision.

A Bind or Pass is written before the card leaves the screen. Two simultaneous likes become exactly one match — never zero, never two.

03 · TALK

Conversation after consent.

Only members of an active match can read or send messages. Unmatch and block end the conversation at the server, not just on your screen.

04 · SAY IT

Your voice, up to a minute.

Voice messages in chat and an optional voice intro on your profile. Recordings live in private storage: a message reaches the two people in that conversation, an intro reaches whoever may already see your profile — nobody else, ever.

05 · CHOOSE

Ten answers, ten filters.

Height, smoking, drinking, drugs, exercise, diet, beliefs, children, wanting children, a car — every one optional, every one filterable. Filters work both ways, so a like never lands in nothing. Your zodiac sign is calculated, never stored.

Built in 2026, behaves like it

An app that respects your attention.

Every feature ships with the boring parts finished: moderation, quiet hours, lock-screen privacy. Because “we’ll add that later” is how people get hurt.

PHOTOS

Six photos, every one reviewed.

Your gallery supports up to six images with explicit ordering — and every new photo passes moderation before anyone else sees it.

NOTIFICATIONS

Push that sleeps when you do.

Quiet hours are enforced on the server, per category, in your timezone. A match at 3 a.m. waits politely until morning.

LOCK SCREEN

Your lock screen stays yours.

Binder never puts message text on your lock screen. “Open Binder to continue the conversation” — that’s all anyone standing next to you will read.

Safety is infrastructure

Not a footer link added before launch.

Binder treats abuse controls as database invariants: authorization, blocks, reports, rate limits, media moderation and deletion are tested where the data actually lives.

Built into the system

  • Server-enforced 18+ onboarding baseline
  • Block dominates discovery, matching, chat — and even queued notifications
  • Profile and message reporting with evidence snapshots
  • Sender-wide messaging rate limits under concurrency
  • Private media storage, moderation and short-lived signed URLs
  • Exact location retained only for server-side distance checks

Your account should remain yours to control.

Privacy, terms and deletion are designed as product surfaces, not hidden support chores.
