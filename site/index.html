Product discipline

The core stays useful without a paywall.

Binder’s early product is intentionally small: identity, discovery, mutual matching, conversation and safety. Monetization comes later only if the free core earns real usage.

01 · DISCOVER

Fit both sides.

Age, preferences and distance are evaluated server-side for both people. Exact coordinates never enter another client’s payload.

02 · BIND

One durable decision.

A Bind or Pass is written before the card leaves the screen. Concurrent mutual likes serialize into exactly one match.

03 · TALK

Conversation after consent.

Only members of an active match can read or send messages. Unmatch and block terminate the server-side conversation boundary.

Safety is infrastructure

Not a footer link added before launch.

Binder treats abuse controls as database invariants: authorization, blocks, reports, rate limits and deletion are tested where the data actually lives.

Built into the system

  • Server-enforced 18+ onboarding baseline
  • Block dominates discovery, matching and chat
  • Profile and message reporting with evidence snapshots
  • Sender-wide messaging rate limits under concurrency
  • Private media storage and short-lived signed URLs
  • Exact location retained only for server-side distance checks

Your account should remain yours to control.
