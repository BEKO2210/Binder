Privacy

What Binder needs — and what it does not expose.

This policy describes Binder’s current beta architecture. It should be updated whenever production data practices or processors change.

Effective: 15 August 2026 · Contact: nullmesh@protonmail.com

Core privacy boundary. Other Binder users do not receive your raw birth date or exact latitude/longitude. Binder derives age and rounded distance server-side.

Data we process

Account: authentication identifiers such as email and Supabase Auth metadata.

Private eligibility data: birth date and current location used to enforce 18+ access and discovery distance.

Profile and UGC: first name, bio, gender, interests, profile photos and messages you choose to create.

Safety data: blocks, reports, report context snapshots, moderation state and abuse-control events.

Device data: an Expo push token may be stored only if notifications are enabled in a configured build.

Why we use it

  • authenticate your account;
  • enforce adult-only onboarding and discovery preferences;
  • calculate candidate eligibility and rounded distance;
  • store and deliver matched conversations;
  • prevent abuse, investigate reports and enforce blocks;
  • operate account deletion and security controls.

Infrastructure

Binder currently uses Supabase for authentication, Postgres data, private file storage and realtime delivery. Expo infrastructure may be used for remote notifications when production push is enabled. Binder does not sell personal data to advertisers.

Location

Binder requests foreground location for discovery. Exact coordinates are stored privately for server-side distance checks and are not returned to another user. Background location is not part of the current product.

Retention and deletion

When you request account deletion, Binder is designed to remove account-associated product data and authentication identity. Certain safety or fraud-prevention records may need limited retention where legally justified; retained records should be minimized and separated from normal product use.

Use the account deletion page or the in-app account controls to initiate deletion.

Your choices

You can update profile information in the app, deny location permission, disable push notifications, block or report another user, unmatch, and request account deletion.
