# Reciprocal Constellation

Reciprocal Constellation is a generative movement about connection as an
agreement, not a prize. Two independent fields move with equal agency. Neither
field is ranked, depleted, or made more valuable by withholding access; a new
form appears only where both sides make the same choice. The viewer should feel
quiet possibility, followed by the clarity of mutual consent.

## Computable principles

1. **Independent agency:** generate two equal populations from separate,
   deterministic PRNG sequences. Each signal follows its own phase, radius,
   speed, and direction; neither population drives the other.
2. **Reciprocal gate:** assign every candidate pair two seeded Boolean choices.
   Draw a connecting bridge only when both choices are true. A one-sided choice
   remains unconnected and is never presented as a near-win.
3. **Shared consequence:** when a reciprocal pair enters its agreement window,
   interpolate a low-alpha bridge and a pulse at its midpoint. The shared form
   belongs equally to both endpoints and exists only after the gate passes.
4. **No scarcity clock:** use continuous, wrapping phases with fixed population
   counts. Signals do not expire, compete, accelerate, or become artificially
   rare over time.
5. **Seeded continuity:** derive every structural parameter from the named seed;
   animation time changes positions and event phase only. The same seed and
   frame time always produce the same constellation.

## Display parameters

- 18 signals per field; reciprocal events recur on seeded 11–17 second cycles.
- Each connection attacks for 0.35 seconds, holds for 0.7 seconds, and decays
  for 1.1 seconds. One seeded reciprocal pair is in its hold window at the
  static/reduced-motion frame.
- The constellation canvas renders at 0.72 opacity. A centred radial mask keeps
  the shared copy axis near 5% strength and restores the field gradually toward
  every edge, so neither side carries more visual weight.
- Behind it, three low-contrast CSS colour fields drift on fixed 36–51 second
  transform cycles. Their blur never animates. The lime field is the smallest
  and faintest; the two dark surface fields carry the composition.
- The blob field and canvas pause together when the document is hidden or the
  hero leaves the viewport. Reduced motion freezes both into a meaningful
  deterministic static composition.

This is Binder's product idea in motion: **both choose, then you talk**. Two
people retain independent agency, mutual consent unlocks the connection, and
nothing in the system manufactures urgency or sells access to possibility.
